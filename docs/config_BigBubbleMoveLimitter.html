Config: BigBubbleMoveLimitter

An object that limits the movement of a player within a BigBubble assigned to it via a BigBubbleGenerator. Creates a differently shaped and sized "plane of restriction" that BigBubble's movement is restricted to, depending on object name.

Arguments

NameTypeGamesRequired?DescriptionValues
Obj_arg0IntegerSMG1True

Move Limitter ID: ID of the BigBubbleMoveLimitter associated with generated bubbles. Nonexistent generators crash the game.

    Obj_arg1IntegerSMG1False

    Attachment Location: Aligns the BigBubble with one of three potential positons relative to the plane of restriction.

    If Obj_arg1 = -1: Attaches the BigBubble to the exact middle of the plane of restriction.
    If Obj_arg1 = 0: Attaches the BigBubble to the outward facing side of the plane of restriction.
    If Obj_arg1 = 1: Attaches the BigBubble to the inward facing side of the plane of restriction.

    • -1: Center
    • 0: Outside
    • 1: Inside
