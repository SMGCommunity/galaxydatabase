Config: WormEatenPlanet

A set of three giant apples with a burrowing green worm connecting them. Initially, the worm is idle and completely invisible. Its appearance and movement are controlled by switches and the cutscene with the name ムイムイ出現. Its initial appearance is controlled by triggering the cutscene nerve with the action name オオムイムイ出現１回目. When SW_A is activated, it starts the action with the name オオムイムイ出現２回目. Triggering the cutscene nerve then causes the worm to move further. Then, when SW_B is activated, it starts another cutscene action named オオムイムイ出現３回目, and triggering the cutscene nerve causes the worm to move even further. Afterwards, the worm comes to a rest.
